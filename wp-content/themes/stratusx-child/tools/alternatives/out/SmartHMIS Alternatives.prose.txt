It’s another hospital operations meeting, and the same software concerns are
back on the table.
A department is still using a workaround for one process. Another team wants a

operations without bringing the same frustrations. That’s where the right
alternatives are worth a closer look.

How to Choose the Right SmartHMIS Alternative

===== PAGE 18 =====
Switching from SmartHMIS is about more than comparing features. Look at
how the new system will fit into the way your hospital already works, what will
be involved in moving across, and what you will actually spend.
Identify Workflow Gaps: Go through the workflows your teams use in
SmartHMIS today. You may find some work well, while others still involve
manual entries, unnecessary delays, or entering the same patient information
more than once.
Check Migration and Implementation: Get the vendor to explain what the
switch will look like in practice. Ask about setup, testing, migration, training, and
when the new system can go live.
Review Integrations: Make a list of the systems you currently use with
SmartHMIS. Check what will happen with your lab, PACS, accounting, payment,
insurance, and other integrations.
Compare Support and Training: Ask what happens after implementation.
Check how staff will be trained and what support they can use when they run
into problems.
Calculate the Full Cost: Do not compare the software price alone. Include
migration, customisation, integrations, training, extra users, and ongoing
support.
Making the Switch from SmartHMIS
Before choosing a SmartHMIS alternative, be clear about what you want to
improve. A clinic may have different needs from a single hospital, while a
growing multi-speciality group may need more from its software.
The six options above cover different types of hospital needs. Compare the ones
that match your setup and, during the demo, run through the work your staff
handles every day. That is a better way to judge usability, data migration,
integrations, and the overall cost of changing systems.
Healthray is one of the options to look at when speciality-specific EMR,
multi-facility management, migration support, integrations, and flexible
deployment matter to your hospital. Overall, the new system should address the
issues that made you look beyond SmartHMIS in the first place.

FAQs
Is SmartHMIS still a good choice for a growing hospital?
That depends on what has changed in your hospital. You may need more
departments, locations, integrations, or workflow customisation than your
current SmartHMIS setup can handle. In that case, compare other options
before you renew or expand the existing system.
How difficult is it to switch from SmartHMIS to another HMS?
Switching can be simple for some hospitals and more involved for others. The
amount of data, number of users, integrations, and customisation in your
current setup can all affect the move. You will also need to plan the data
migration, workflow changes, staff training, testing, and support around go-live.
What should happen to historical patient records after switching
from SmartHMIS?
Talk about your old records before you sign with the new vendor. Confirm which
records will be migrated, which ones will stay archived, and how you will access
older records later. Also ask how the transferred data will be checked.

===== PAGE 20 =====
Can a hospital use a new HMS alongside SmartHMIS during the
transition?
A phased transition can be possible, depending on the systems involved and the
vendor's migration approach. Some hospitals may need a period where the old
and new systems are used together while data, workflows, and staff processes
are tested.
