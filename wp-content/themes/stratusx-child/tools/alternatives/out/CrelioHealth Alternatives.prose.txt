5 Best CrelioHealth Alternatives &
Competitors in 2026
As sample volumes increase, coordinating the work between collection and
report delivery gets harder. Staff collect and barcode a sample, send it for
testing, and match it with analyser results. Staff are checking pending tests,
following up on results waiting for approval, and making sure reports are sent
out on time. When this is happening across a high number of samples every day,
small delays can quickly turn into extra work for the team.
It gets harder when the lab starts handling work from more collection centres,
adding new analysers, sending some tests to outside labs, or managing more
referring doctors. There is simply more to track across the same workflow, and it
becomes easier for a sample, result, or report to get held up.
That is when lab owners start asking whether their current LIMS still fits how the
laboratory operates. For a lab using CrelioHealth, the search for an alternative
is less about finding the software with the longest feature list and more about
finding one that fits its workflow, integrations, reporting needs, and growth
plans.
This guide compares five CrelioHealth alternatives for 2026 and looks at what
each platform is built to handle, where it has limitations, and which laboratory
requirements are worth checking before making a switch.

How to Choose the Right CrelioHealth Alternative
Before choosing a CrelioHealth alternative, start with the way your lab works
right now. Look at your daily sample volume, collection process, branches and
collection centres, and the analysers or other systems you already use. Also
make a note of the parts of your current setup that create the most manual
work or slow things down.
Then find out what switching will actually involve. Inquire about the vendor’s
plans for migrating data, training staff, implementing the software, support,
security, and even customisation for your lab if required. Also be clear about the
pricing beyond just the basic subscription.

===== PAGE 18 =====
Before you make the final call, ask each shortlisted vendor to show the software
using a few situations from your own lab. See how the staff would handle
registration, samples, results, reports, billing, or other everyday tasks, and how
much manual work is involved. It is also worth checking whether the software
can handle the next stage of your growth, such as more tests, instruments,
collection centres, branches, or B2B work.
Making the Switch from CrelioHealth
Finding a CrelioHealth alternative usually starts with a simple question: what is
no longer working well for your lab? It could be the way samples move through
the lab, reporting delays, limited integrations, branch management, or the
amount of manual work your team still handles.
The five LIMS compared above approach these needs differently. Before
choosing one, look at the workflows that matter most to your lab and make sure
the software fits them without adding unnecessary steps or requiring major
changes to how your team works.
Healthray is one option to evaluate for labs looking for connected lab
workflows, analyser and system integrations, quality management, migration

areas you want to improve and the kind of setup your lab plans to have going
forward.
FAQ’s
What happens to our existing CrelioHealth data after we switch?
You can ask the new LIMS provider to move your existing data, but first find out
exactly what they will take across. Old patient records, reports, test masters,
billing information, and other data may not all be handled in the same way. Get
this clarified before you start the migration.
Will our lab have to stop work while moving to a new LIMS?
Your lab can keep running during the change in many cases. The new system
can be prepared in the background, and the final move can be planned for a

suitable time. Ask the vendor what will happen on the day you go live and
whether any part of your normal work will be affected.
Can our existing report formats and test masters be moved from
CrelioHealth?
Some can be moved or recreated, but do not assume everything will come
across exactly as it is. Show the vendor your current report formats and test
setup before you sign up. They can then tell you what they can transfer and
what will have to be configured again.
What happens to samples and reports that are still pending
when we switch?
Make sure these are part of the migration discussion. Samples still in process,
reports waiting for approval, unpaid bills, and similar open work need a clear
handover plan. Otherwise, your team may have to check two systems to work
out what is still pending.
Can we use CrelioHealth and the new LIMS at the same time?
You can discuss a short overlap period with both vendors. This can be useful
while the team gets familiar with the new system, but decide which software will
be used for new registrations, samples, billing, and reports during that period.
Otherwise, the same work can end up being entered twice.
