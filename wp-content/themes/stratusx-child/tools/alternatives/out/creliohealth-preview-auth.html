As sample volumes increase, coordinating the work between collection and report delivery gets harder. Staff collect and barcode a sample, send it for testing, and match it with analyser results. Staff are checking pending tests, following up on results waiting for approval, and making sure reports are sent out on time. When this is happening across a high number of samples every day, small delays can quickly turn into extra work for the team.

It gets harder when the lab starts handling work from more collection centres, adding new analysers, sending some tests to outside labs, or managing more referring doctors. There is simply more to track across the same workflow, and it becomes easier for a sample, result, or report to get held up.

That is when lab owners start asking whether their current LIMS still fits how the laboratory operates. For a lab using CrelioHealth, the search for an alternative is less about finding the software with the longest feature list and more about finding one that fits its workflow, integrations, reporting needs, and growth plans.

This guide compares five CrelioHealth alternatives for 2026 and looks at what each platform is built to handle, where it has limitations, and which laboratory requirements are worth checking before making a switch.

How We Evaluated CrelioHealth Alternatives

We looked at all five CrelioHealth alternatives using the same checklist. The focus was on how each platform handles everyday lab work, from patient and sample management to collection centres, barcode and phlebotomy, analyser integration, results, reports, and sharing. We also looked at areas that matter when a lab is growing, such as ease of use, B2B and referral work, customisation, reporting, implementation, migration, scalability, support, security, pricing, and India-specific requirements.

We started with the vendors’ own websites and product documentation. We also checked G2, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest, TechJockey, and Google reviews where they had useful information. The details in this comparison were checked in September 2026.

We looked at how well each platform fits Indian diagnostic and pathology lab operations, rather than judging it only on the number of features listed. Ratings were one part of the research, not the basis for choosing an alternative. We compared the software for its actual workflows, ease of use, the kind of lab it can support, and the areas worth checking before a switch.

When a vendor had not published clear information about a feature, integration, price, or compliance requirement, we did not treat it as confirmed.

How to Choose the Right CrelioHealth Alternative

Before choosing a CrelioHealth alternative, start with the way your lab works right now. Look at your daily sample volume, collection process, branches and collection centres, and the analysers or other systems you already use. Also make a note of the parts of your current setup that create the most manual work or slow things down.

Then find out what switching will actually involve. Inquire about the vendor’s plans for migrating data, training staff, implementing the software, support, security, and even customisation for your lab if required. Also be clear about the pricing beyond just the basic subscription.

Before you make the final call, ask each shortlisted vendor to show the software using a few situations from your own lab. See how the staff would handle registration, samples, results, reports, billing, or other everyday tasks, and how much manual work is involved. It is also worth checking whether the software can handle the next stage of your growth, such as more tests, instruments, collection centres, branches, or B2B work.

Making the Switch from CrelioHealth

Finding a CrelioHealth alternative usually starts with a simple question: what is no longer working well for your lab? It could be the way samples move through the lab, reporting delays, limited integrations, branch management, or the amount of manual work your team still handles.

The five LIMS compared above approach these needs differently. Before choosing one, look at the workflows that matter most to your lab and make sure the software fits them without adding unnecessary steps or requiring major changes to how your team works.

Healthray is one option to evaluate for labs looking for connected lab workflows, analyser and system integrations, quality management, migration support, and multi-centre operations. The final decision comes down to the areas you want to improve and the kind of setup your lab plans to have going forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to our existing CrelioHealth data after we switch?

You can ask the new LIMS provider to move your existing data, but first find out exactly what they will take across. Old patient records, reports, test masters, billing information, and other data may not all be handled in the same way. Get this clarified before you start the migration.

Will our lab have to stop work while moving to a new LIMS?

Your lab can keep running during the change in many cases. The new system can be prepared in the background, and the final move can be planned for a suitable time. Ask the vendor what will happen on the day you go live and whether any part of your normal work will be affected.

Can our existing report formats and test masters be moved from CrelioHealth?

Some can be moved or recreated, but do not assume everything will come across exactly as it is. Show the vendor your current report formats and test setup before you sign up. They can then tell you what they can transfer and what will have to be configured again.

What happens to samples and reports that are still pending when we switch?

Make sure these are part of the migration discussion. Samples still in process, reports waiting for approval, unpaid bills, and similar open work need a clear handover plan. Otherwise, your team may have to check two systems to work out what is still pending.

Can we use CrelioHealth and the new LIMS at the same time?

You can discuss a short overlap period with both vendors. This can be useful while the team gets familiar with the new system, but decide which software will be used for new registrations, samples, billing, and reports during that period. Otherwise, the same work can end up being entered twice.
