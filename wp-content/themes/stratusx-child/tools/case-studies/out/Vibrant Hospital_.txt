[H2] **A busy hospital facing operational challenges**
[P] Vibrant Multi Speciality Hospital provides emergency, outpatient and surgical care across multiple departments. The hospital is NABH accredited and provides 24/7 emergency care. 
[P] As its operations grew, managing the administrative side became increasingly difficult. Billing, inventory, and patient workflows relied on manual processes and disconnected systems, leading to delays, errors and unnecessary administrative work. Staff were spending significant time on these activities, while leadership was hesitant to replace systems handling critical patient, inventory and billing data.
[H2] **Four operational challenges were slowing the hospital down** 
[P] The main challenge at Vibrant Hospital was not simply having different tools, but the manual work and fragmented processes required to manage information across departments. 
[H3] **01 – Separate Systems**
[P] Different departments relied on separate systems for patient, billing and inventory activities. Information was spread across platforms, making coordination and visibility more difficult. 
[H3] **02 – Billing Took Days**
[P] Staff had to enter billing information manually, verify details and collect data from different systems. This stretched billing cycles over days instead of hours and delayed the process. 
[H3] **03 – Limited Stock Visibility**
[P] Staff lacked clear visibility into medical supplies, making it harder to maintain stock levels. Some supplies were over-ordered while others ran short, leading to wastage and unnecessary purchases. 
[H3] **04 – Heavy Administrative Work**
[P] Staff spent considerable time on paperwork and routine administrative tasks. This increased their workload and reduced the time available for their core responsibilities.
[P] These challenges highlighted the need for a more connected way to manage daily hospital operations.
