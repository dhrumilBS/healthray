[H2] **Diagnostic lab workflow under pressure**
[P] Jeevan Rekha Hospital provides emergency care, inpatient and outpatient services, along with speciality treatments and diagnostic services. As diagnostic volumes increased, the laboratory team had to manage more samples, results and reports as part of its routine work.
[P] Tracking samples, entering results, checking information and completing reports required additional time and effort. Processing delays, data-entry errors and repeated checking made these tasks more difficult and slowed down report completion. The team also had to spend more time resolving errors and following up on pending work.
[H2] **Four challenges affecting Jeevan Rekha's diagnostic lab**
[H3] **01 – Sample tracking gaps**
[P] Tracking samples across different testing stages was difficult. The team spent additional time checking sample status and following up on pending samples.
[H3] **02 – Result entry errors**
[P] Manual result entry increased the risk of incorrect or missed information. Errors required additional checking and correction before reports could be completed.
[H3] **03 – Report turnaround delays**
[P] Higher diagnostic volumes made it harder to complete reports within the expected time. Delays in processing and verification increased report turnaround time.
[H3] **04 – Limited team capacity**
[P] The existing laboratory team had to handle increasing sample volumes without additional staff. This limited the team's ability to take on more diagnostic work.
