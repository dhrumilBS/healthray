[SUBTITLE] **– THE HOSPITAL**
[H2] A Multi-Specialty Hospital With Complex Care Workflows
[P] Jabalpur Hospital & Research Centre (JHRC) has multiple specialised departments, supported by pathology, radiology, pharmacy, physiotherapy, and critical care. It also offers fertility care, dialysis, and cardiac care.
[P] JHRC brings together specialised departments along with fertility care, dialysis and cardiac care. The hospital’s broad service mix brings clinical and support teams together around the same patient journey. The care journey spans these functions.
[SUBTITLE] **– THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEMS**
[H2] Four Challenges Made Workflows Harder To Manage
[P] As patient care and hospital administration moved across departments, four areas created friction in JHRC’s day-to-day work. The issues affected staff and coordination across teams.

    NESTED TABLE (2 rows x 2 cols)
    [row 0 cell 0]
        01 – Patient Data Access
        Patient information had to be accessed across different stages of care, making it harder for teams to find the right details from consultation through treatment, pharmacy, admission and discharge.
    [row 0 cell 1]
        02 – Diagnostic Information
        Pathology and radiology reports needed to reach treating doctors and remain linked to patient records. Any delay in sharing this information could slow down the next step in patient care.
    [row 1 cell 0]
        03 – Billing, Pharmacy & TPA
        Billing, pharmacy and TPA teams handled related patient and treatment information. Working through separate processes meant more data entry and more follow-ups between teams.
    [row 1 cell 1]
        04 – Management Visibility
        Management needed timely information on patient activity, billing and departmental performance. Getting this information from different areas made it harder to track hospital operations.
----- END TABLE 3 -----
