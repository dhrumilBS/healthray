[H2] **A growing hospital facing administrative challenges** 
[P] BBMH provides advanced laboratory and imaging services, ICU care, cath lab services, and multiple clinical and surgical specialities, along with 24×7 emergency services. As its patient volume increased, managing the hospital's internal processes became harder.
[P] Information was spread across paper files, spreadsheets and different systems. Administrative teams spent considerable time searching for information, tracking data and handling follow-ups, while doctors were also pulled into administrative work. 
[H2] **Four challenges affecting BBMH's hospital operations**
[H3] **01 – Scattered Information**
[P] Patient records, billing information and reports were spread across paper files, spreadsheets and different systems, making information harder to find.
[H3] **02 – Manual Workflows** 
[P] Billing and record-keeping involved manual processes and repeated cross-checking, making routine administrative work more time-consuming. 
[H3] **03 – Limited Visibility** 
[P] Management did not have timely access to information about hospital activities. Reports took time to compile, making it harder to get a clear view of operations. 
[H3] **04 – Administrative Burden** 
[P] Internal coordination and follow-ups were taking doctors and teams away from their core responsibilities. 
[H2] **Why BBMH chose Healthray** 
[P] BBMH needed to move away from scattered records and manual processes while keeping billing, patient information and reporting connected. It chose Healthray HMS to centralise these workflows, reduce administrative follow-ups and give teams faster access to operational information.
