[H2] **Managing Multiple Hospital Workflows** 
[P] Lotus Hospital provides medical and surgical care, supported by dialysis, radiology, laboratory and pharmacy facilities. Its radiology department offers digital X-ray, sonography, colour Doppler and CT services.
[P] As the hospital managed these activities across different teams, staff needed timely information to allocate beds, schedule procedures, hand over patient updates, and monitor medicine stock. Gaps in these workflows could slow down routine operations and make important updates harder to track.
[H2] **Four Workflow Gaps Affecting Daily Operations**
[H3] **01 – Bed status gaps**
[P] Staff had to check whether beds were occupied, available or ready during admissions and transfers. Delayed bed-status updates could slow allocation.
[H3] **02 – OT scheduling conflicts**
[P] Teams had to manage theatre availability and procedure timings across four operating theatres. Schedule changes could lead to conflicts between procedures. 
[H3] **03 – Missed nursing updates**
[P] Important patient updates could be missed during shift handovers when information was passed between nursing teams, leaving incoming staff without key updates. 
[H3] **04 – Late expiry tracking**
[P] Pharmacy teams had to monitor medicine expiry dates across stock. Late identification of near-expiry medicines left less time to act.
[H2] **Why Lotus Hospital chose Healthray**
