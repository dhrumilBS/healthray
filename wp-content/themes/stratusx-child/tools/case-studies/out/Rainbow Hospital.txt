[SUBTITLE] **– THE HOSPITAL**
[H2] How a Day Runs at Rainbow Hospital
[P] A busy day moves through four hands – the admission desk, the operating theatre, the billing counter and the records room. Each one holds the next. When a bed opens late or a file goes missing, every hand down the line waits with it.
[P] That coordination was being done on phone calls, whiteboards and paper files. It held while volumes were small, but at this scale the gaps started to cost beds, theatre hours and staff time the hospital could not spare.
[SUBTITLE] **– THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEMS**
[H2] Where the Floor Was Getting Stuck
[P] Four daily workflows were quietly draining capacity.
