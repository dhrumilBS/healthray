[SUBTITLE] **- THE CLINIC**
[H2] The Care Setup At Lilavati Clinic
[P] At Lilavati, diagnostics, imaging, physiotherapy and an in-house pharmacy sit alongside specialist consultations. In a single visit, a patient might see a doctor, get an investigation done, pick up a report, then use another service on the way out.
[P] So the day only runs if teams stay in sync. The doctor needs earlier records to hand. The diagnostics team needs the exact test details. And the patient needs the prescription and report without a wait or a follow-up call.
[SUBTITLE] **- THE CLINICAL PROBLEMS**
[H2] Where The Clinic Workflow Was Getting Stuck
[P] Four recurring workflows consumed staff time each day.

----- TABLE 3 (1 rows x 1 cols) -----
[row 0 cell 0]
    NESTED TABLE (2 rows x 2 cols)
    [row 0 cell 0]
        **01 - Routine Requests**
        After a visit, patients contacted the front desk for prescriptions, reports and follow-up details — around 120 calls a day, close to one full role spent on the phone.
    [row 0 cell 1]
        **02 - Follow-Up Calls**
        Staff made reminder and follow-up calls. At a 21% no-show rate, nearly one in five specialist slots stayed empty, requiring the team to rearrange the day.
    [row 1 cell 0]
        **03 - Clinical Notes**
        Each speciality had different documentation needs. Handwritten notes added minutes to each consult and made records harder to read across departments.
    [row 1 cell 1]
        **04 - Test Hand-Offs**
        Test orders moved through several teams before reaching the lab, so staff had to confirm manually that each order had been received.
----- END TABLE 3 -----
