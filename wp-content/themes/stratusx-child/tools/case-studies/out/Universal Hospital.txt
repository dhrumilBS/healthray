[H2] **A hospital facing coordination challenges across departments**
[P] Universal Hospital provides diagnostic, treatment, surgical, chemotherapy and transplant services across multiple departments. Its infrastructure includes critical care units, CT/MRI facilities, an in-house blood bank, a cath lab and a modular operation theatre for transplant procedures.
[P] These services require teams to share patient information and work across departments. Paper-based documentation and disconnected departmental systems made information harder to access, while surgical teams manually tracked OT schedules, pre-operative approvals and post-operative updates. These processes created delays and made it harder for teams to work efficiently. 
[H2] **Four operational gaps affecting hospital care** 
[H3] **01 – Paper-heavy documentation**
[P] Staff relied on paper records and manual documentation, increasing administrative work and physical record storage. Doctors also spent more time managing records.
[H3] **02 – Disconnected departmental systems**
[P] Departments used separate software, leaving patient information scattered across systems. Duplicate entries and delayed updates made patient history harder to access.
[H3] **03 – Manual surgical coordination**
[P] OT schedules, pre-operative approvals and post-operative updates were handled manually. This made it harder for surgical teams to keep procedures and related updates aligned.
[H3] **04 – Manual patient onboarding**
[P] Patient registration involved manual data entry and verification, adding extra steps before patients could proceed with care. 
[H2] **Why Universal Hospital chose Healthray**
[P] Universal Hospital needed a more integrated way to manage its clinical and administrative workflows. The hospital chose Healthray to connect patient records, laboratory, imaging, pharmacy and billing workflows in one system.
[P] “Morning OPD is usually very busy, so the system needs to be fast. I need old consultations, reports and medicines quickly while seeing the patient. Healthray shows all this on one screen, and even when lab or pharmacy updates come in, they reflect in the same patient record, so I don’t have to ask staff for old files. It saves my time during consultation.”
